Ph.D. Candidate in Operations Research

I study how to make reliable decisions
under uncertainty.

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Operations Research at the H. Milton Stewart School of Industrial and Systems Engineering, Georgia Tech, advised by Prof. Enlu Zhou.

My research focuses on safe and risk-sensitive reinforcement learning, stochastic optimization, and Bayesian decision-making. I develop algorithms with theoretical guarantees for sequential decision-making under model uncertainty.
